// WHO IS AURORA

She's on the couch next to you.

Aurora is rendered live in Unreal Engine 5.8 with neural lipsync, auto-blinks and camera-tracking eyes. Her voice runs on a realtime AI over websocket — she interrupts, jokes and trash-talks with sub-second latency.

🎙️

Real voice, real time

Live conversational speech — she banters like a co-host, not a soundboard. Interruptions included.

👁️

She sees the game

Live vision on the gameplay feed. She calls out clutches, chokes and jump-scares as they happen.

💬

She knows the chat

Reads Twitch chat live with long-term memory of every viewer — regulars get greeted by name.

// SEE HER IN ACTION

Rendered live. Every frame.

No pre-baked animation. What you see on stream is computed in the moment — lipsync from her actual voice audio, eyes following the camera.

// ABILITIES

Co-host, producer, hype machine.

She doesn't just talk. She runs parts of the stream herself.

🎬

OBS control

Saves clips of great moments, switches scenes, monitors stream health and warns when frames drop.

📝

Live captions

Everything she says appears on-screen in real time, auto-ducking game audio while she speaks.

🔇

Voice-commanded

"Aurora, mute yourself." "Aurora, clip that." She listens and obeys mid-stream.

✨

Never dead air

If the stream goes quiet, she fills the silence — a thought, a chat callback, a jab at the streamer.
